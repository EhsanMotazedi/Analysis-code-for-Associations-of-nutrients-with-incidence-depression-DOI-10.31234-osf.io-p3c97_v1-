The R code used for the statistical analysis, 5 Poisson regression models and multiple imputation sensitivity analysis, to investigate 
associations between various nutritients and incidence of depression in two large cohort studies in the Netherlands. Full details can be
found in:
"Associations of a healthy dietary pattern, foods, and nutrients with the incidence of high depressive symptoms 
in two large cohort studies in the Netherlands" (DOI:10.31234/osf.io/p3c97_v1)
